# PCS bakeoff assumptions

- Candidate focused commands have completed successfully and retained their default `outputs/latest.json` before aggregation.
- Candidate validators are part of the input trust boundary; the aggregator reruns each validator.
- The C20/C30 shared 4,096-element, one-point geometry controls only public polynomial size. It does not align complete relation work, soundness, masking, transcript, or verifier cost.
- Single-run time and RSS observations must not be ranked as benchmarks.
- C40 source-watch evidence has no proof measurement.
- Frozen v0.3/H0 is the only faithful relation control because no SP-10 compression candidate passed.
- An upstream proof measurement is never a PQTC measurement unless the exact relation, corpus, public statement, transcript, codec, and verifier are integrated; none are integrated here.
